# AcmeCloud Deployment Guide

## Public SaaS
The default deployment is multi-tenant SaaS operated by AcmeCloud. Business and Enterprise customers can restrict inbound access by approved IP ranges.

## Private networking
Enterprise customers can connect through private networking in the United States and European Union regions. Private networking is not available on Starter or Business plans. A private connection normally requires two to five business days after network details are approved.

## Dedicated deployment
A dedicated single-tenant deployment is available only under an Enterprise agreement. It requires a minimum one-year commitment. AcmeCloud operates the control plane, while customer data is isolated in a dedicated environment. Dedicated deployments have a typical provisioning lead time of three weeks.

## Releases
The public SaaS service receives weekly production releases. Dedicated deployments can select a two-week delayed release channel. Critical security patches may bypass the delayed channel when AcmeCloud determines that immediate remediation is required.
