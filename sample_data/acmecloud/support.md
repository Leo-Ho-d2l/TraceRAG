# AcmeCloud Support Policy

## Support channels
Starter customers use community support. Business customers can open priority support tickets through the console. Enterprise customers receive priority tickets plus a designated support escalation path.

## Response targets
For severity-one production incidents, Business has a four-hour initial response target. Enterprise has a one-hour initial response target. Severity-two incidents target one business day for Business and four business hours for Enterprise.

## Maintenance
Planned maintenance that could materially affect service is announced at least five business days in advance. Emergency maintenance may occur without the standard notice when required to protect security or service integrity.

## Service reviews
Enterprise customers may schedule quarterly operational reviews covering incidents, usage, reliability trends, and planned architecture changes. Business customers receive self-service monthly usage reports but do not receive scheduled quarterly reviews by default.
